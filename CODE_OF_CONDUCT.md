# Community guidelines

Help make Music Lyrics Bar welcoming to people with different experience levels.
Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give feedback on
work rather than people. Questions, failed experiments, and beginner contributions
are welcome. English and Korean may both be used in community conversations.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, spam, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable. Respect copyright and do not post
complete lyrics, private Music caches, or account credentials.

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions.
Maintainers may ask participants to edit a post, remove disruptive content, lock a
conversation, or restrict participation according to the situation. Good-faith
technical disagreement is welcome.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for sensitive concerns.
Do not publish private details or accusations in a public issue. For routine
moderation, flag the relevant conversation to a repository maintainer. GitHub's
own community rules also apply.
