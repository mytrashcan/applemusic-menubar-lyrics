## Lead with the mechanism

Apple Music already downloaded the lyrics. Music Lyrics Bar reads that local copy and shows the current line in your Mac’s menu bar. Timed words brighten as they’re sung. https://github.com/mytrashcan/applemusic-menubar-lyrics

## Lead with the annoyance

Missed a lyric while reading? I made a Mac menu bar app so I can glance up and keep my place. Open the song’s lyrics in Apple Music once, then hide Music. https://github.com/mytrashcan/applemusic-menubar-lyrics

## Lead with the limitation

What happens when a Mac app gets all its lyrics from Apple Music’s private cache? No API key, and no lyric if the recording isn’t cached. Here’s what it looks like when it matches. https://github.com/mytrashcan/applemusic-menubar-lyrics
